Confirmed specification clarifications: no reversal or pullback signals are generated before the first three M15 candles from market opening have closed because trend is NONE. Reversal and pullback confirmation waits expire at the end of the fifth M5 candle, counting the entry-tick candle as the first; candle identifiers are opening times (09:43 entry, 09:40 first candle, 10:00 fifth candle, 10:05 expiry). Confirmation must occur strictly before expiry; at the deadline the window is closed, so the 10:00 candle closing at 10:05 cannot confirm a setup expiring at 10:05. Same-direction signals have no effect on an open position; at most one position may be open and no per-zone trade cap is currently required. A fresh exit that satisfies pullback conditions creates a new pullback opportunity and is evaluated anew with its own breakout window. Re-entry without a fresh valid exit continues the prior opportunity only until its existing deadline and does not renew it; a new valid exit is required for a pullback after that window closes. The market's starting location identifies which input zones to consider initially; it does not redefine trend calculation. Historical data is available for indicator warm-up in backtests and live operation. At startup inside a zone, pre-startup touches/exits are not reconstructed; the system waits for a new touch. Confirmation/valid-exit precedence before expiry remains open. Implementation conformance remains unverified.

The UTC trading-day boundary is 00:00; positions may cross it, but the configurable maximum holding time defaults to 6 hours from position opening. Close at the earlier of that deadline or 10 seconds before scheduled market close; new entries and pending LIMIT orders are disallowed at the market-close cutoff. The reference Forex schedule is Sunday 17:00 to Friday 17:00 `America/New_York`, converted dynamically to UTC (22:00 in EST, 21:00 in EDT); exact broker/symbol schedules and holidays override it. Failed-close handling remains open. Implementation conformance is unverified.

The account currency is USD. `RemainingDailyMaxLose` uses a rolling 24-hour window: sum recorded loss amounts with event timestamps in `(t-24h,t]` and subtract from `max(60 USD, 3*PerOrderRiskableMoney)`; there is no calendar reset and wins do not offset recorded losses. Whether recorded loss amounts include costs remains open.

Risk sizing is independent of remaining loss budgets: reject the order when its final-volume estimated risk exceeds any applicable budget; do not shrink the trade to fit remaining budgets. Actual-fill risk may exceed the estimate without corrective action or volume reduction. The initial fallback 5R TP price stays fixed throughout the position.
